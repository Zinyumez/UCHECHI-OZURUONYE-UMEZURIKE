Employee Attrition & Workforce Performance

An Excel dashboard bringing together employee attrition and workforce measures.

Project overview

What this project covers

The supplied Excel dashboard screenshot contains an attrition split by active and exited employees, KPI cards for attrition and employee count, and charts for attrition by job role, income and gender.

Observations from the supplied material
  • The dashboard separates current employees and employees who have left.
  • Job role is used as a dimension for comparing attrition.
  • Monthly income is compared by attrition status.
  • The dashboard includes a gender and attrition comparison.
